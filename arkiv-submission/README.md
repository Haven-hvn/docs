## Decision

**Track: the open `Other` lane. Deadline 31 August 2026, 23:59 UTC.**

- **AI & DevTools closed on 11 August** — three days before we looked. Unavailable.
- **Marketplaces is open until 20 August** but Haven has no listings, quotes or bookings;
  entering it would trigger the rubric's own 1-point anchor, "the track label is incidental."
- **DeFi opens 21 August** and does not describe Haven either.
- **`Other`** asks only that an idea genuinely needs queryable, time-scoped, tamper-proof
  entities — which is the question Haven answers best, and it has the longest runway.

## The pitch in one paragraph

A community's archive stays encrypted on Filecoin and unlocks from a token or NFT balance.
Arkiv is the registry that says which archives exist, who published them, and who may open
them right now — a **public index of private things**. Everything that is a byte, a secret
or a payment lives on another network, and is named as such.

## Scope note

The submission pitches Haven's **index layer**, not the whole protocol. That keeps track
alignment sharp, puts the thing that breaks without Arkiv at the centre of the pitch, and
gives a first slice that is genuinely a weekend's work.
